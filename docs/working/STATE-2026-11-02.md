## The lane: one system, two sources (the driver's law)

- **Dynamic is the base, always.** `own lane + LaneInsetPerRadius / aimRadius` metres, no speed factor, signed by a two-heading cross, gated to aim radii under `LaneDynamicRadius`. It exists for "the inside is the shorter path".
- **The field is a shaped exception.** At the apex the *trace* is the product (speed is irrelevant there), so the precomputed field overrides the dynamic ask **only** on nodes the builder stamped `TrackPoint.LaneShaped`.
- **The shaped region** is the apex backwards to where the growth stops (`LaneShaped` is set only by the backwards loop in `BuildLaneField`). The 180-node outside plateau and the forward exit hold are **deleted** — nothing past the apex is written, so nothing post-apex can override the dynamic ask.
- **No blend.** The shape begins at the node after the builder stopped writing, at full strength.
- Apex identity: one gate, `ApexAheadOfLookahead` — the same predicate selects the corner and forgets it.
- `AbsoluteLaneEnabled` is the A/B switch in `AutosportRacingSystem.cs`; it is **true**.

## Open defect (the driver's latest report)

The lane follows the black spheres **too soon**: the drawn lane is already inside before the spheres are. Cause: the field is sampled at the lane's preview node (`speed × LanePreviewSeconds`, one second ahead) and then used as a *current* ask, so the car is told to be where the shape will be a second later. The shape should be sampled where the car is (or at the pursuit's aim node), while the dynamic ask keeps its preview lead.

## Decisions made this session

- Projection is a **speed offset**, not a pedal one: the target bleeds at `ProjectionSlowdownMphPerSecond`, floored at `max(OffTrackSpeedFloorMph, bend speed − OffTrackSpeedSlackMph)`, and it never touches a pedal.
- The projection's outside test has **no bend gate and no direction gate** — off the road is off the road, either side.
- Off-track throttle is a **floor**, not a cap (`OffTrackThrottleLevel`), skipped under countersteer and inside a stuck recovery.
- A node's `.Angle` is **not** a direction; take sides from two headings a span apart (`TrackTurnSign`, deadband + latch). Five runtime sites switched; `TrackLoader`'s two remain.

## Next: the turn-in start from geometry (open)

The cubic Hermite is in (build 1606) with `LaneFieldShapeNodesPerRadius` as its one free number, and the driver has ruled that the free number is wrong in principle: the start of the turn-in must fall out of the **radius and the width**, because the line has to be a constant arc touching the **outside** before the apex and the **apex** itself.

Proposed derivation (symmetric corner):
- `r_line = (R − w) + 2w/(1 − cos(θ/2))` — the existing `OptimalRadius` closed form, `w` the half width, `θ` the corner's total turn.
- the outside tangency sits `θ/2` before the apex, so the transition length along the line is `L = r_line · θ/2`, in nodes `L / spacing`.

Sanity: R 50 / 90° / 12 m road gives 85 m radius and 67 m of run-in; R 200 / 20° gives 984 m and 172 m.

Open question: an asymmetric corner (entry turn ≠ exit turn) breaks the symmetric assumption — the outside tangency must be placed from the entry angle, not from θ/2. Check against the drawing before building.

## Lane and brakes as they stand (build 1640)

- The lane shape is a smoothstep, symmetric about the centre of the road: entry from the outer edge to the apex, exit from the apex back out. Length = `1.75 · traverse / tan(crossing)`; the crossing angle is `ShapeAngleScale × atan(traverse / radius)`, tuned by the Development row (0.50–1.00, default 0.75).
- The shape is honoured **only before the apex**; past it the dynamic lane takes over. It is gated on `LaneShaped` + `LaneShapeApex == held apex`, and the held corner is released only when the car is past `LaneShapeExit`.
- The outer end of a shape is clamped by `LaneShapeOuterMarginMetres` on the outside only; the inside may touch the edge.
- Five seconds of outside hold before an entrance (`LanePrep`, 6 s, capped at `LanePrepOutsideCapMetres` beyond the car), driven by its own radius-free, speed-gated lookup.
- **Entrance** now means `TrackPoints[apex].LaneShapeStart` everywhere — the line, the brake target and the learner's sample all resolve through `CornerEntranceNode`.
- Brake learner: one sample at the entrance, `error = carSpeed − planSpeed`; `step = error ≤ 1 m/s ? (1−error)·0.02 : −error·0.02`; skid penalty and apex-miss penalty are **disabled by const**, not deleted. The factor is per racer *and* per corner.

### Open critique (not implemented)

The factor map is `(car, corner)` — one scalar per pair, seeded at 0.75 ± jitter, so a car carries one unknown per corner instead of one unknown for its brake model plus small per-corner residuals. Suggested: learn a per-car base, keep the map as a ±0.15 residual, drop the seed jitter from the learned value, add a deadband and outlier rejection, and shape the update as the implied decel ratio `(v_t/(v_t+e))²` rather than an additive step.
