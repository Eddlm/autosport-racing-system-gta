| H1 | REAL | `CornerSpent` fires on any car past an entrance with \|long G\| < 0.05 g, with no test that the car is actually *driving* the corner. | H1b | PARTIAL | The Gs signal is a 200 ms mean, so the release can fire up to 200 ms late on a braking onset and up to 200 ms early on a brake release. | DataStructures.cs:15-16 (`AccelWindow = 10`, `AccelIntervalMs = 20`), :31-40 (`AverageAcceleration`), Racer.cs:3091-3100 (sampling) | 10 × 20 ms = 200 ms window. | H2 | PARTIAL | `CornerSpent` cannot fire for a corner that has no shaped entrance and no `StartNode`… but it also *cannot* fire when the entrance is more than half a route length behind, because `MetresToNode` wraps there. | Racer.cs:2172-2182 (`CornerEntranceNode` falls back apex → `StartNode` → `OffsetCornerNode`, never −1 for a valid apex); Racer.cs:2768-2769 (wrap at `RouteLengthMeters * 0.5f`) | On a short circuit (route < ~800 m) an entrance 250 m behind reads as +150 m ahead → `> 0f` → returns false → the corner is never released by the coast path, only by `HasPassedApex` (count/2, Racer.cs:4131). | H3 | REAL | The lane's release is a no-op for the corner it just released, because the replacement candidate is chosen by `NearestApexAhead`, which knows nothing about "spent", and re-adopts the same corner while its apex is still beyond `max(speed × 1 s, 20 m)`. | Racer.cs:729 `candidate = NearestApexAhead()`, :732 release condition, :735 `_laneHeldCorner = candidate`; Racer.cs:1171 `reach = max(speed × LanePreviewSeconds, LaneApexMinReachMeters=20)`, :1172 `ApexAheadOfLookahead` | At 60 km/h (16.7 m/s) the reach is 20 m. K4 in VERIFY-NOW ("the shape stops once the car is through the entrance") is therefore only observable on short corners. This is the mechanism the 09-11 verification saw as working. |
