## How a row is marked

- **VERIFIED** — read in the code/files today, or reproduced from the artefacts named in the row.
- **PROBABLE** — deduced from what was read; the mechanism is certain, the occurrence is not observed.
- **NOT RE-OPENED** — the report's own row was not re-checked in this pass. Used only for negative findings, where there
  is no claim to falsify. A row is never marked closed on the strength of a commit message.
