# AUDIT 31 — steering / lane / pedal hot path

**Surface**: the four systems edited in the 48 h before `85ba39b` and never audited since — (1) the corner line, `ComputeCornerLane` and the blend clock (`Racer.cs:1370-1678`); (2) the lane mover, `UpdateLaneMover` (`Racer.cs:1252-1362`); (3) the Gs lane throttle floor and **Open Up on Throttle** (`Racer.cs:2192-2204`, `:2495-2536`, `:2609-2621`); (4) the brake learner, its entrance mark, skid gate, air flag and apex notice (`Racer.cs:2724-2961`). Cross-couplings across those four are the point of the card, so they are collected in §5 rather than listed per system.

**Date**: 2026-10-27.

**Build audited**: **1277**, source `HEAD 85ba39b` ("corner line: the blend is timed over the span it actually travels, not the whole gate", whose body records `build 1277`). `bin/Release/ARS.dll` and `Dist/AutosportRacingSystem/ARS.dll` are byte-identical, sha256 `b56d79b671a8567069cfbec4ff022d47b9ee5d814fca28743e6c9e8a273c85a6`. **The live install was not located in this pass** — no `Scripts\AutosportRacingSystem\` under `/c`, `/d`, `/e` or `/f` within a four-level walk — so the card's statement that the deployed DLL matches `bin/Release` is taken as given, **not** re-verified here.

**Method**: read-only — no edit, build, deploy, commit or game run. Evidence is the source at `85ba39b`, `git show` of the four corner-line commits and the two constants' commits, and the two documents that describe the system (`AGENTS/AGENTS-TECHNOTES.md`, `docs/steering-and-pedals.md`), which are themselves in scope. **VERIFIED** = read in the code, or arithmetic on values read from the code. **PROBABLE** = one step rests on runtime state no artefact here records. Nothing in this report is driver-verified.

**Verdict**: **no blockers.** Nothing found breaks a shipped default or corrupts a value silently. The five release-notes split three ways: one unfinished goal in the corner line (R1), one rate law bypassed by a feature that ships off (R2), and three in the learner's reporting and control — the menu copy names the wrong quantity (R3), the air and floor verdicts are collected and never printed (R4), and a mid-race toggle parks the verdict queue (R5). Ten further rows are file-level: dead state, dead guards, stale comments and two latent hazards. The sharpest finding is **R1**: `85ba39b` set out to make phase 1 land on the end distance, and it does — but only at constant speed. On the braking corner the commit was written for, the phase still completes early by roughly the ratio the car slowed, which puts the car on the inside extreme about 2.3x further from the apex than the geometry asks. The document trail is worse than the code: `AGENTS-TECHNOTES.md:135-185` describes three brake-learner laws that no longer exist, with their retired constants, plus a notice grammar (`| floor off`, `| skid`, `| air`) that the code never prints (F5, R4).

## 1. The corner line — what the code does now

The gate is `blendStartMeters = max(2 × halfWidth × 6, 20)` (`:1484`) and the end is `blendEndMeters = 2 × halfWidth × 1` (`:1485`), both in metres of route from **the car** (`:1474-1476`), with `steerRefPoint.TrackHalfWidth` supplying the width. The outside bound is `safeBound - 0.5 - bias` and the inside extreme is `-safeBound` in the `cornerDir` frame (`:1620`), so the demand runs from `+outsideBound` at phase 0 to `-safeBound` at phase 1, and `_apexInsideNow` (the guard `UpdateLaneMover` obeys at `:1261`) arms on the first tick the demand is inside the car (`:1657-1665`).

Three state machines sit on top, each keyed by apex node and each released differently: `_linePhaseLatched` (the ratchet, `:1583-1603`), `_linePhaseDir` (the handedness latch, `:1600`, `:1606`), and `_lineCornerNode` (the picker's stickiness, `:1440`). The ratchet is released only when a **different** apex is adopted (`:1583-1588`), deliberately not when the window flickers (`:1608-1610`); `Launch` clears all of them (`:2420-2434`), and `_lineBiasNode` with them. That is coherent, and the 5 s reseed block (`LinePhaseReseedBlockMs`, `:215`, `:1586`) does what its comment says.

`blendStartMeters` and `blendEndMeters` can never invert: `blendStartMeters = max(6 × fullWidth, 20)` and `blendEndMeters = fullWidth`, so `blendStartMeters ≥ 6 × fullWidth > fullWidth = blendEndMeters` for any positive width, and `blendSeconds` is always computed from a positive span. `blendSeconds` is Infinity before the gate — `_lineClockMps` is 0 and the guard at `:1552` has not fired — and `blendElapsed` is 0 in the same ticks, so `0 / ∞ = 0` and there is no NaN. That path is safe by construction, not by a guard.

What is not safe by construction is R1: the clock measures time, the geometry measures distance, and the two agree only while the speed is constant.

## 2. The lane mover — the leash and the reversal

`LaneMoverAccelMps2` 40 and `LaneMoverSpeedCapMps` 24 are halved for `OutsideLine` (`:1322`, `:1326`), doubled on acceleration for an overshoot (`:1325`, never on the cap), and cut to `_leanApproachMps` for the lean (`:1329`). The leash starts at 2 m and takes the cap to 0.8 by 4 m (`:1244-1246`, `:1333-1334`) and never reaches zero, which is what its comment claims. The arrival term is `sign(gap) × min(cap, sqrt(2 × accel × |gap|))` (`:1340`) and is added to the request's own rate, clamped apart (`:1338-1341`) — the gap can always close while the line moves, as designed. The reversal teleport (`:1298-1308`) requires both a sign flip in `gap × _laneRate` and both ends on opposite sides of the car by more than 0.1 m, and `_laneOvershoot` (`:1315`) is what makes `aimLane` fall back to the car's own lane at `:878`.

Two things in this function are worth keeping an eye on and are reported above rather than argued: the NaN guard's position (F6) and the stale 1.0 s justification for the acceleration (F4d). Neither is a defect in the law.

## 3. Gs lane throttle floor and Open Up on Throttle

**Open Up**: the card asked for proof that it cannot reach the throttle cap or the HUD share. Half of that holds and half does not.

- It **cannot** reach the throttle cap. `OpensUpSlipScale()` reads `Control.Throttle` and returns a scalar (`:2196-2204`) used at exactly one site, `_clampTargetDeg *= OpensUpSlipScale()` (`:2294`). `_slip.InnerPct` — the operand of the pedal closure at `:2555-2558` — is a measurement and is never touched. The `x<share>` token in the readout is `SteerCeilingFactor × SlowCornerSlipShare` (`:4172`), also untouched.
- It **does** reach the readout's primary number. `ratio = asked / clamp` uses `_chasingClampDeg` (`:4169-4173`), which carries the throttle scale because the chase is driven by the scaled target (`:2294-2310`). On the same line `_slipFactor` (`:4174`) is **unscaled**. At half pedal with the item at 40 %, the readout shows `ratio` inflated ~2.5x beside `factor 1.00` — "the guard is holding the wheel back" printed next to "the guard is not cutting". The theory-mode readout (`:4158-4166`) is immune, because both its operands are scaled.

**Gs floor**: the composition chain is honest — `min(floor, Control.MaxThrottle)` so TCS/stability/overspeed/rival/chill/yield/offtrack/recovery all outrank it (`:2617`), a full stand-down under countersteer (`:2616`), and a full stand-down while the off-track clock runs (`:2506`). `_gsFloorActiveThisTick` is `Control.Throttle > throttleBeforeFloor` (`:2620`), i.e. it means "the floor raised the pedal", not "the floor's regime is live" — which is the right meaning for its only reader, the brake learner's entrance latch (`:2864`), and the comment there gets the tick ordering wrong (F3). What the chain does not preserve is the slew (R2).

## 4. The brake learner — the mark, the gate, the verdict

The mark is a **position**, measured against a frozen bar: `_brakeSampleApexSpeed` is set once when the sample opens (`:2761`) and `apexSpeed = _brakeSampleApexSpeed + BrakeLearnerBarMps` is the accept bar (`:2816`), where `BrakeLearnerBarMps = FullPedalSpeedErrorMps × BrakeErrorMultiplier = 3` (`:300`). The mark is taken on the first tick below and confirmed by two more (`:2823-2841`), and the metres are signed from the corner's entrance (`:2833-2837`). The verdict lands `BrakeCommitDelaySeconds` 0.75 s after the apex pass (`:2892-2908`), so the exit's slide is inside the sample. The step is `-metres × 0.02` capped at ±0.25 (`:2944`), the factor lives in `[0.5, 1.5]` (`:451`, `:470`) and starts at `0.8 ± 0.05 (driver) ± 0.05 (corner)` (`:385-389`, `:513`). `BrakeLearningApplies` is a `const true` (`:402`), so the two `if (BrakeLearningApplies)` guards (`:2926`, `:2946`) are unconditional writes in disguise.

The skid gate does what it says: `_brakeSampleMaxSlideDeg` is only accumulated while `HasPassedBrakingTarget() && !HasPassedApex && Control.Brake > BrakeSkidBrakeShare 0.25` (`:2872-2877`), and above the gate `maxSlide > TRLateralAtSpeed × 2.5` throws the position verdict away for the flat 0.1 (`:2917-2928`). Both sides of that comparison are degrees (the same `TRLateralAtSpeed` the parked ceiling feeds to `PeakSlipCeilingAt`, `:2229`), so the units agree.

The air flag is where the card's description and the code part company: it is not "report only", it is not reported at all (R4), and the sampler that feeds it is the only reason `ApexBrakingSpeed` is walked every 100 ms per car. The two dead guards at the method's tail (F1) are the fossil of the block that used to consume it.

## What could not be verified

- **The live install.** No `Scripts\AutosportRacingSystem\` found under `/c`, `/d`, `/e`, `/f` within four levels; `bin/Release` and `Dist` are identical by hash, so the header's build claim rests on the card and the commit body, not on my own read of the deployed DLL.
- **R1 in the car.** The arithmetic is exact but the entry speed of a real corner is not recorded; the `Corner line` log has the two numbers needed (`d` and `demand`) and no run was made.
- **F6 and F8 reachability.** Both need a runtime trace (a NaN `_laneRequest`; a speed flicker across the 0.2 m/s accept bar) that no artefact here contains.
- **Whether the 2.5x skid multiple and the 0.25 brake share are calibrated.** They are internally consistent and match the tyre curve's plateau (`docs/steering-and-pedals.md:105-118`); nothing in this pass can say whether they fire at the right moment on track.
