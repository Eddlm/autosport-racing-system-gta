## The seven live blockers, ranked

1. **02-B2** — a race started from the Race menu inherits the previous race's grace timer and ends itself on it. The first menu path a player meets.
2. **05-P2** — the player's car keeps `SET_OVERRIDE_NITROUS_LEVEL` after a race, or after Nitrous is switched off mid-burn. It changes the player's car outside the race.
3. **04-W2** — when the clamp-space corridor inverts, both rival walls are discarded and the whole road is returned. The ordinary three-wide band (3.3-5.2 m) is exactly where it fails, and it fails silently.
4. **03-G2** — one hot entry cuts a corner's brake factor with no ceiling per event; at the 0.5 floor the car brakes about 90 m early for every remaining lap, and recovery is 14 clean laps.
5. **03-G1** — the route curvature's brake cap is inert on every lap after the first one that arms a corner, because `RequiresBraking` is a latch.
6. **05-P3** — every apex target and braking solve is built from the grip sampled at the car, so the next corner inherits this corner's downforce and the current surface.
7. **04-W1** — the wall block still decides from a 500 ms record; the rival *list* moved to 250 ms, the record values did not, and 500 ms is 18 m at 130 km/h against a ~5.5 m overlap window.
