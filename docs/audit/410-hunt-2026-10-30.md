**What this is**: the working ledger for a six-hour, unattended defect hunt across eight themes. Method, per theme: one
read-only subagent reviews a narrow scope; the parent re-reads every claim in the source before anything is touched
(a subagent's finding is a claim, not a fact); only real, local, behaviour-neutral defects are fixed; one commit per
theme. Design changes are **not** made — they are filed as decisions for the driver.

### The one question the reviewer could not settle, and its answer

It flagged that finding 1 becomes a blocker **if** `ScriptSettings.GetValue<T>` throws on a malformed value, because the
reads sit in the constructor with no `try`. Decompiled `libs/ScriptHookVDotNet2.dll` (`ilspycmd -t GTA.ScriptSettings`):
`GetValue<T>` wraps `Convert.ChangeType` in `try`/`catch` and **returns the caller's default**. A garbage numeric cannot
throw at load — it silently reverts, which is a quieter defect, not a crash. Finding 1 stays `release-note`.

| 4 | The projection's throttle cap is applied after `ComposeThrottleCap`, so `MaxThrottle` and `ThrottleReasonLevel` report a pedal that never existed | **REAL** | **FILED** (D-T6-4) |
| 5 | `MaxBrakeFromRoute` is written below three early returns, so it keeps its last value and a `FinishedRace` car inherits 0.2 for ever | **REAL** | **FILED** (D-T6-5) |
| 6 | The countersteer brake cap is hard-min'd to 0.5 before the glide, so `MaxBrakeFromCountersteer` can never bind | **REAL** — a floor under a floor | **FILED** (D-T6-6) |
| 7 | One normaliser (`capShare`/`available`) feeds the steering cut and the throttle cut, and `SteerCeilingFactor` scales it | **BY DESIGN** — the register says Peak Slip Share *drives* the measured-slip limiter, and that the normaliser feeds the throttle cut is the documented reason a second scaling is forbidden | no action |
| 8 | `ApplyStuckRecoveryOverride` writes both pedals outside the pipeline, bypassing the slew and every cap, and reports `ThrottleReasonLevel = 0` while the pedal is −0.5 | **REAL** | **FILED** (D-T6-7) |

without track data; "scale the clamp target only, never the shared normaliser" is satisfied at `:1696` — but that output is the
dead one (finding 1) — while `SteerCeilingFactor` does to `capShare` what that rule forbade, which the register settles as by
design; `capShare` has a single assignment, so the "second writer" residual is **CLOSED**.
