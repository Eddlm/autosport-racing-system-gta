## SHVDN build compatibility (release-critical)
- **The asi and the API dll are a matched build pair and no version check can see a mismatch** — both builds' v2 dll reports `ScriptHookVDotNet2 2.11.6.0` while the bridge (`SHVDN.*`, in the asi's own managed assembly) changes member by member. ARS needs `ScriptHookVDotNet2.dll` from the same build as its asi, or a foreign one loads, resolves as "API version 2.11.6" and dies at the first bridge call with `MissingMethodException`; **compile-clean proves nothing**.
- **`VerifyScriptBridge()` (`AutosportRacingSystem.cs:375`)** probes `Game.GenerateHash` after the log banner, logs the cause (`forced: true`) and rethrows, so a bad install is diagnosable rather than silent. **Order is load-bearing: no native call may precede it, and none may sit in a static field initializer** — a static initializer cannot be reported by a guard that has not run.
- **The public 2022 bundle cannot run on a current game at all** (every script dies), so a SHVDN build matching the game version is a prerequisite ARS cannot supply. **Never ship a pinned `ScriptHookVDotNet2.dll` in Dist** (it would manufacture that mismatch); a stray `ScriptHookVDotNet3.dll` is harmless, and the v3 migration is the exit from this trap.
- **A second distribution — SHVDN Enhanced (SHVDNE)** — satisfies the matched pair *by construction* and un-deprecates the v2 API; ARS's whole v2 surface resolves against it. **Static analysis only, never run in game**, and it would falsify our published install instructions: `AGENTS-SHVDN.md`.

2. `ComputeTargetSpeed` (`Racer.cs:1150`) — builds the intended speed; see the Speed pipeline.
3. `ComputeSteering` (`Racer.cs:391`) — resolves the lane into a target offset and sums the authority chain into `Control.SteerDegrees`. **`_debugLaneAimPoint` never feeds the steering error** (its only reader is the debug line), so the error is always a **distance in metres, never a bearing** — there is no pure-pursuit angle in the live chain.
4. `ApplySteerLimits` (`Racer.cs:924`) — the two side limits closed by a single `Clamp`, and it runs **last among the writers of the steering request**; the ceiling is the corner geometry, or a share of the live peak slip angle while **Peak-Slip Steer Cap** (AI Settings) is on. **A steering writer added below it is a silent bypass** — the bug class this shape removes.
5. `ConvertSpeedToPedals` (`Racer.cs:942`) — one `[-1,1]` combined input, split, capped and slewed. **GridWait lives in three places** (`ComputeTargetSpeed`, `ProcessAI`, `TranslateSteerToInput`) — keep them in sync.
6. `TranslateSteerToInput` (`Racer.cs:1122`) — smooths steer toward target, then remaps degrees → −1..1; the countersteer rate doubling is **live and never validated in game**.

## The steering authority chain (`ComputeSteering`)
In order, each term feeding the next: **course error** is velocity-referenced (`-SignedAngle(trackDirection, courseDir)`) and deliberately kept **separate** from the lane steer, because merging the two oscillated. Then off-track recovery, the **lane steer** (a cross-track P on `targetLane − DeviationFromCenter`, blended with the projected read, plus rival repulsion **added** to the bias and never overwriting it), the side-by-side heading match (**one-way** — it engages only while the overlapping rival is moving toward this car's side, `Racer.cs:564`), and finally the **outer PD** — the loop's only rate feedback, so **it is load-bearing: with it off the cars limit-cycle around centre**.

**No term is gated**; `ApplySteerLimits` and the slew rate only cap the output. Full analysis, and why the aim-error PID was tried, driven and **abandoned** in favour of this chain: `AGENTS-STEERING.md`.

## Lane systems — where on the track the car aims
Resolved in `ComputeSteering`, later overriding earlier: **high-speed line** (`Racer.cs:565`, always runs — the inside edge of the track's own curvature, binary rather than graded and suppressed inside a deadband), **corner outside approach** (`Racer.cs:590`, a temporary outside hold), **avoidance** (`Racer.cs:659`), then **`ApplyRivalWalls`** (`Racer.cs:724`) clamps the result between track bounds and rival walls.

**Lane systems only *set* the target lane; how hard it is pursued is the authority chain's job.** The track bound is the raw half width — the *centre* is the bound and the box may overhang — which is why `OutOfTrackDistance` (`Racer.cs:2003`) and the recovery's safe edge are centre-based too.

- **Two steering lookaheads, only one of them named — do not conflate them**: `ComputeHighSpeedLane`'s chord is anonymous, while `SteerLookaheadMinMeters` (`Racer.cs:113`) floors the *other* reference, `LookAhead.SteerRef`, which feeds the course error, the track half-width every lane bound is measured against, and the debug aim point.
- **Corner approach engages only when the car arrives already above its own grip-derived speed for that apex**, so an easy corner suppresses itself simply because the car is not above that speed. Three things suppress it outright — close-corner, chicane and an entrance-direction gate — and **the hold decision can flip to "hold" once per approach but never back**.
- **The corner-approach hold is coupled to route speed**: route speed governs a sweeper's speed, and the hold only engages when the car arrives above its own grip-derived speed for that apex, so a sweeping corner suppresses its own hold. That regime is no longer switchable — the Route Speed Limit toggle went in `70e177c` — so the lever that shifts it is **Route Offset (mph)**.
- **Corner-commit maneuvers** replace the outside hold with a lane beside the target rival on the corner's inside, and clean up when their apex is passed. The corner line phases are a **hard switch**, no lerp.
- **The lane P is deliberately uncapped**: one flat degrees-per-metre gain on the lane error, halved for the move out to the outside, so the approach tapers itself as the error closes. **Do not "fix" an aggressive lane move with a ceiling** — a ceiling flattens the top of the P into a constant-command phase that builds lateral velocity the short tail cannot shed, and that *is* the overshoot; the gain is the lever (`AGENTS-TECHNOTES.md`).

## Speed pipeline — how fast the car goes
`Intention.Speed` is `min(cornerSpd + Corner Offset, followTrackSpd + Route Offset)`, then clamped by `MaxSpeed` and the off-track projection cap. **The offsets are split, one per authority, and each is applied inside the `min`** — so Corner Offset also lifts apex-limited speed (and `CorneringSpeedLimit`, `Racer.cs:1438`) while apex speed itself stays pure physics (`AutosportRacingSystem.cs:2417`).

- **`cornerSpd`** — the kinematic braking plan toward the apex speed over the distance to the apex node (`Racer.cs:2433`). Its decel (`Racer.cs:2469`) is a grip-limited base **plus the gravity component of the mean grade over the braking span**, sampled along the same trimmed span the solve integrates.
- **`followTrackSpd`** — route curvature from a time-based lookahead window (`Racer.cs:2394` sampled, `Racer.cs:2772` the law), the authority for sweeping corners, and **always in the `min`**: the Route Speed Limit toggle that could drop it to 999 was removed in `70e177c`, replaced by the additive Route Offset. Its one live modifier is the **`SteerLimitedSpeed` blend** (`Racer.cs:1420`, 70% steer-implied speed to 30% route speed) and it is fed the *clamped* steer of the previous pass, so a tighter steer ceiling **raises** route speed.
- **Slope grip loss is LIVE** (exponential pitch model on corner + route speed) — ignore any older note claiming otherwise. The hill/crest grip floors are tuned as one knob; read the code for current values, and note the known issue that the model reads an upcoming hill and brakes before the car reaches it.
- **Pedal gain is one linear map, no feedforward** (`Racer.cs:135`), with the brake side divided by `BrakeErrorMultiplier` on top because braking is deliberately the softer side. Both pedals then ramp to that target at a fixed slew rate.
- **Off-track projection cap (`Racer.cs:1029`)** caps the combined input by how far off-centre the projection lands, ramping across the whole half-width to a shallow floor. It is applied as a `Min`, so it never cuts braking — but a negative cap **is** a brake command over the plan's own throttle.
- **The throttle/brake avoidance override was REMOVED.** **Asymmetry is by design** — corner is the loose target, route the tight one, so don't "balance" them with flat offsets; on high-grip cars the route term wins.

## Corner lifecycle (apex table + four held targets)
1. **Apex table at generation** — `BuildApexTable` (`TrackLoader.cs:229`) walks the track in chunks and takes each chunk's smallest precise radius as its apex, keeping only the node, radius and a tentative 1g speed. **The close-corner rule drops near-duplicates and flags the mid-range ones `SuppressOutsideApproach`**, and **no radius flag survives**: the racer decides at the window instead.
2. **Held apex queue per racer** (`Racer.cs:2267` `UpdateApexLeapfrog`, `Racer.cs:2303` `RefillApexQueue`) — targets persist until passed, then leapfrog and refill, and the queue invalidates on a route-data change or a low-speed incident. Per-car apex speed is physics, `√(grip · gravity · radius)`.
3. **Braking map** — each held apex feeds a kinematic solve over the usable distance and `cornerSpd` takes the lowest demand. The buffer is a flat time × speed; pressure does **not** scale it.
4. **Route-speed split** — route speed governs sweeping corners while the four-apex map handles tight ones from far away, and **min governs**. **Handover is positional and unconditional** when the braking target is passed, and the apex plan is released only once braking is genuinely done.
5. The old live rolling and far-killer scans are **superseded**, but `Brain.Corner` is **live again**: `CommitApexQueue` (`Racer.cs:2373`) re-instances it each tick from the nearest held apex, so outside-approach, corner-commit and chevron consumers read live state.

Track facts: **1 node = 1 m**; circuit lookaheads use modulo and point-to-point clamps.

## Grid car selection (pace-matched)
- **Model stat natives need an int hash, not a spawned handle** — that is what makes pre-race evaluation free, with no car in existence. The spawned-handle natives are *different functions*, so don't mix them up; the hash table is in `AGENTS-TECHNOTES.md`.
- **The roster is one flat hand-editable file** — `Scripts\AutosportRacingSystem\Vehicles\cars.txt`, one model key per line, `#` comments ignored (`VehicleCatalog.cs:17`). `BuildPowerCache` (`AutosportRacingSystem.cs:447`) hashes each line and **rewrites the pool in place to canonical hash-string keys**, which every consumer is keyed by; the `arsbuild*` cheats only *append* and refresh the roster live.
- **Threading contract**: the load thread only *discovers* the pool by pure file I/O, while `BuildPowerCache` reads the natives on the **main thread**. **Calling GTA natives on the background thread hard-crashes the game.**
- **Pace score (`AutosportRacingSystem.cs:97`) is a raw per-model number, not 0–1**, and the leaderboard prefix is the same metric the grid ranks by. Electric models are scored at the midpoint of the game's own drive-force ramp, with the flag from a verified native.
- **Selection (`VehicleSelector.cs:12`) is a ranking, not a window** — one pool pass ranks every paced model by distance from the target, takes the closest N with a shuffled tie-break, and returns model keys without touching a file. `SelectHardcoded` (`VehicleSelector.cs:42`) is the full-roster test bypass.
- **Force-Fill Grid (Settings → General Settings, `ForceFillGrid`) recycles the models that actually spawned, round-robin, until the grid target is met** (`AutosportRacingSystem.cs:3651`) — a one-car pool like `tests.txt` fields a full grid of it, and a model that spawned nobody is never retried, so a broken roster line costs its load wait once.
- **Grid slots are built to order and the count must include the player.** `BuildGridSlots` (`TrackLoader.cs:475`) runs again for `Racers.Count` right before `GridBuilder.Place` (`GridBuilder.cs:17`) because `Place` reads **two slots past each car**, and `AddPlayerToGrid` (`AutosportRacingSystem.cs:1920`) adds the player *after* the grid was sized — `470a1a1` shipped exactly that crash, where the setup threw and throttle never started the countdown.
- **Pace Mode is the sole authority on the anchor (`759a36a`)**: Relative resolves from the player's pace, Absolute from the persisted target, and **every** start path resolves from the mode with no override of its own. `PowerTargetScale` has exactly two writers — its own menu item and `RefreshPowerControls` (`AutosportRacingSystem.cs:1236`).
- **Menu vocabulary**: the items are `Grid PI Mode` / `PI Offset` / `PI Target` while keys and code keep the name pace. **`PaceTarget` is a seed, not a preference** — `RefreshPowerControls` clamps it to the live fleet span on every menu build and saves the result, so do not reconcile a live-vs-Dist difference in it.

## Sideloaded fleet handling
**The grid's cars are custom and their handling is sideloaded, not in the game's own meta**: `VSLooseStream.asi` mounts loose FiveM-style resources from the game install's `Sideload\` folder, each declaring `data_file 'HANDLING_FILE'`. **ARS reads the live `CHandlingData` from memory, never these files** (`VehicleMemory.cs`), and it reads the per-racer handling values once per racer at racer init, so a mid-session edit moves the car without moving those reads.
**`Sideload\0_vsl-handling\handling.meta` is the file of record and the only declaration of these names** — the per-car `Sideload\<car>\...\handling.meta` copies were emptied to stubs, their contents kept beside them as `*.bak-vslpriority` and **never to be restored**: while those duplicates existed, boot could serve a stale generation of the shared file and only a save mid-session landed. It is a **hand-balancing** surface, judged by driving rather than by ARS — tuned at LSIA, settled at NURBGP. **Its `<strHandlingFlags>` is a numeric hex string parsed straight into `hFlags`** (`handlingMgr.cpp:1699`), and nearly every car sets `TYRES_CAN_CLIP`, which *raises* the wheel's compression cap so the tyre sinks into the ground when bottoming out (`wheel.cpp:1359–1367`) — so a car that looks sunk or clips is a per-model flag, never a player-vs-AI difference (`AGENTS-VANILLA-STEERING.md`).

## Per-racer state
- **Aggression** (grid-assigned by position, first lowest to last highest; the player stays mid) scales the avoidance buffer and allowed TCS wheelspin. **Pressure** is proximity × aggression, rising slowly and falling quickly, and it drives divebomb/defend/arming.
- **Stability-aware grip is absent, not neutral** — `AvgGroundStability` was removed and `CurrentMechanicalGrip` (`Racer.cs:2945`) has no stability factor at all. A trustworthy wheels-off-ground detector is still the prerequisite, and the throttle-side `WheelsOnGround` rules are the only live part.
- **Maneuvers are cards** (`Maneuver` on the racer, no legacy arm blocks): a card **plays** into the slot and **folds** on its own condition, priority **ChillOut → DefendLane → DiveBomb → Yield**, with **Nitro slotless**. A played card is **never reconsidered mid-play** — unplaying causes thrash.
- **ChillOut** halves throttle and holds station behind the closest rival ahead until the field thins, arming only above a minimum speed so a slow car cannot become a roadblock. **DiveBomb** targets the closest reachable rival with a deeper braking target while its card lives.
- **DefendLane** covers the inside against a faster chaser, and **Yield** lets a faster overlapping rival by near the entrance. The corner-commit lane holds for the card's whole life, ignoring the latch, release timer and `SuppressOutsideApproach`.
- **Nitro** has four situations (contested / defended / lonely / finish-spender) behind vetoes on braking, off-track, big steer angle and low gear on high-geared RWD cars, plus the **fairness gate** (AI arms only if the player has nitro). **AWD is exempt** via the handling struct's drive bias; full rationale in `AGENTS-TECHNOTES.md`.
- **Passengerize** shifts AI drivers to the passenger seat while a rival overlaps, so they cannot swerve into contact, and is never applied to the player. **Ghosting was removed** in the avoidance cleanup and must be rebuilt later.
- **The player's car and the AI's get different engine systems** — the special ability writes a real per-wheel grip multiplier and a faster steering ramp while it slows the world clock, `Automobile.cpp`'s player-only block adds a sideslip auto-centre, and the AI side gets free ABS and time-sliced wheel collisions; ARS neither detects nor blocks any of it (`AGENTS-VANILLA-STEERING.md`).

**Duel model (designed via Council, not implemented) — full design in `AGENTS-DUEL.md`.** Physics-aware card play on one shared time-to-apex primitive taken from each car's own live plan; **anything about rivals, overtaking or card play starts in `AGENTS-DUEL.md`.**

## Debug (LemonUI Debug submenu)
Toggles live in `Menu-Debug.ini` and the load loop walks `DebugToggles.Keys` (`AutosportRacingSystem.cs:179`), so **retiring a toggle retires its key**; menu order is not declaration order and the menu reads persisted values. **Show Inputs** owns the three projections plus the applied pedal trail, while **Track Analysis** owns the lane-aim line to `_debugLaneAimPoint` with wall stubs at the steering reference node — both draw off the **debug-focus racer**, never the player. Retiring a toggle retires its key and new toggles **append**; `ShowProjection`/`ShowInputTrail` are the dead pair, and `ReverseRoute`'s dev key has no effect yet must stay declared or the repair pass fights it on every launch.

## Leaderboard (frozen results board)
`DrawLeaderboard` (`AutosportRacingSystem.cs:1756`) is gated by its toggle, and **positions freeze per racer** on crossing the line via `LeaderboardFinish` (`AutosportRacingSystem.cs:201`) mirrored into `RacePosition`. Finishers draw in locked order, the player's row is yellow, and the finish block awards `RaceReward`, sets `RaceStatus = Finished` and calls `CleanEverything` (`AutosportRacingSystem.cs:1354`) — so the HUD only draws during Countdown/InProgress. Draw order and the two **write-only** fields (`LapTimes`, `VehicleData.TextPerformanceIndex` — neither has a reader) are in `AGENTS-TECHNOTES.md`.

## Durable gotchas — do not "fix" these
- **`if (1 == 2) return;` is an INVERTED gate — it disables nothing**, because the return only fires when the condition is true and it never is. Correct idioms are a bare `return;` or the block form; this bit the start-line flare disable (`bdd1b29`) for weeks.
- **Remap with a descending output range + `clamp=true` is inverted** by `Clamp` when `min > max`, and NaN compares less-than-anything. Keep output clamps ascending and use a descending *input* range for a reversed map.
- **NaN discipline**: `Clamp(NaN, -limit, +limit)` returns the min bound, i.e. instant full-lock — guard steering outputs and any clamp input that can be non-finite. Never hand a non-value sentinel to a *seeding* getter either, or it writes the sentinel into the ini.
- **A null-check on a freshly `new`-ed object is dead code.** Two "cannot find file" popups lived behind `if (new XmlDocument() == null)` while the real failures *throw*, so those paths have **no** error handling.
- Synchronous setup work can pause `OnTick` and temporarily suppress per-frame debug visuals; make it incremental if that matters.
- **`Handling.Downforce` reads `0x0014` in `VehicleMemory.GetDownforce` (`VehicleMemory.cs:34`) and the code is right** — an earlier note claiming `0x0010` was a transcription slip off an off-by-one source line number. With the right field the principled grip divider matches the engine's own formula, so don't bring back the old curve-fit hack.
- **Oversteer and sliding are different quantities, and what a steering law can see depends entirely on its reference vector** — never state a reference's consequences as general properties. The live course error is velocity-referenced, which is the *blind* one: for a pure body rotation it reads as perfectly tracked **while the car is sideways**, and the slide authority is the blend instead.
- **The aim-error PID / measured-yaw-rate steering law was tried, driven and ABANDONED — the pre-PID chain was restored in preference to it.** Do not resurrect it blind; the durable lesson is that **it had no cross-track term at all**, so its standing line error could only be fought with P. Full record in `AGENTS-STEERING.md`.
- **Offroad gravity needs a baseline reset before the multiplier** — `Initialize` (`Racer.cs:323`) runs every race and on respawn, so setting gravity immediately before the offroad multiply is load-bearing or it stacks across restarts.
- **Speed asymmetry is intentional** (see the Speed pipeline) — don't "fix" it. **`Intention.SteerLimitedSpeed` is live** (`Racer.cs:1420`, blended 70/30 into `followTrackSpd`), and it is fed the *clamped* steer of the previous pass — the older note here claiming it was write-only was wrong.
- **`Options` enum values are positional**, so retiring a member renumbers the rest and one must never be persisted or exchanged as an int. Nothing does today, but re-check before any numeric consumer appears.
- **`World.DrawMarker` has a per-frame render budget**: markers past it are **silently not drawn** and draw order decides who loses, so long debug geometry belongs in `DRAW_LINE` instead. The budget is shared with every other marker the script draws that frame, so a visual can break because something *else* got greedy.
